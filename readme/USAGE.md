## Getting started

1. Open the **Knowledge** app from the Odoo home screen.
2. Click **+** next to *Search articles* (shortcut `Alt+C`) to create an
   article, then give it a title.
3. Click **Edit** (`Alt+E`) to start writing. Type `/` for the command menu:
   headings, lists, tables, quotes and more. Click **Save** (`Alt+S`) when you
   are done, or **Discard** (`Alt+J`) to throw away your changes.

## Organising articles

All of the actions below are in the **⋯ (More actions)** menu at the top right
of an article, unless noted otherwise.

- **Sub-article**: hover over an article in the sidebar and click its **+**
  (*Add a sub-article*).
- **Rename**: rename the article.
- **Move to…**: pick a new parent, or **Top level**.
- **Tags…**: add or remove tags. Create tags under
  *Configuration → Tags*.
- **Duplicate**: copy the article.
- **Print or export PDF**: print the article or save it as a PDF.
- **Archive**: hide the article from the tree. To bring it back, turn on
  *Archived articles* in the sidebar filter, open the article and choose
  **Restore**.
- **Sort and filter**: use the buttons next to the **Articles** heading in the
  sidebar.

## Finding things

- Click **Search articles** at the top of the sidebar to open the search
  palette.
- Tick **Search inside content** to match article text as well as titles.
- Use the arrow keys to move through results and **Enter** to open one.

## Working together

- **Comments**: write below the article. Use **Reply** to start a thread.
  `Ctrl+Enter` posts.
- **Favourites**: the star adds an article to the *Favorites* section of the
  sidebar.
- **Likes**: the heart shows appreciation. Like and view counts appear at the
  bottom of the article.
- **Chatter**: open the article from *Configuration → Articles* to see its
  full tracking history.

## Versions

Every saved change to the content creates a new version. Open
**⋯ → Version history** to:

- pick any version from the list
- compare it with the current article or with another version
- use **Swap** to reverse the direction of the comparison.

## Sharing outside Odoo

Click the **link** icon (*Copy public link*) at the top of a published article.
Anyone with the link can read it at `/knowledge/article/<token>` without
logging in. Unpublishing the article disables the link.
